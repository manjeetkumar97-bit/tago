Privacy Policy

App: Tago GPS Map Camera (com.tago.gpsmapcamera) · Developer: Manjeet Kumar, Delhi, India · Effective: 26 October 2026

Tago GPS Map Camera takes photos and videos and stamps them with the place, time and a QR code so they can be used as site evidence. This policy explains what the app uses, where that information goes, and the choices you have. In short: your photos, videos and notes stay on your phone. We have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers that store your data.

1. What the app uses and why

  • Camera – to take photos and videos.
  • Precise location – to stamp the photo with its latitude/longitude, address and GPS time, and to match AFTER photos to their BEFORE photo. Location is used only while the camera is open.
  • Microphone – to record sound with videos. If you tap the microphone to speak a note, your phone's speech service (for example Google) turns your voice into text.
  • Photo location (media location) – to read the location saved in your own photos, for example to show them on a map.
  • Notes, project/site names and team code – typed by you and printed on your photos.

2. Where your information is stored

Photos, videos, PDF reports and the capture records used by "Verify" are saved on your phone (Pictures, Movies and Documents folders). Settings are saved inside the app. Nothing is uploaded to us. Uninstalling the app removes its settings; your photos stay in your gallery until you delete them.

3. Information that leaves your phone

To work, the app sends a small amount of information to these services:

  • Address lookup – the photo's coordinates are sent to your phone's geocoding service (Google Play services) to get the address printed on the stamp.
  • Map thumbnail – to draw the small map on the stamp, the app downloads map tiles for the area around you from OpenStreetMap. This reveals the approximate area and your internet address to the map server. You can turn the map thumbnail off in Settings.
  • QR code on the photo – the QR holds the photo's coordinates, Photo ID and GPS time. When anyone scans it, their browser opens our verify page (hosted on GitHub Pages), which shows those details and a "See on maps" button. Anyone who has the photo can read this information.
  • Things you choose to do – sharing a photo, opening Google Maps, or speaking a note sends that content to the app or service you pick, under that service's own privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone for advertising or any other purpose.

4. Your choices

  • Allow or deny camera, location and microphone in your phone's Settings at any time.
  • Turn the map thumbnail off in Tago Settings.
  • Delete any photo, video or report from the app or your gallery.

5. Children

Tago is a tool for adults and professionals. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

6. Security

Information sent to the services above travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data on your phone is protected by your phone's own security, such as its screen lock.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if paid features are added), this page will be updated and the date above changed.
